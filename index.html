How to play

🎲 Throw two sticks (0–3 each); add them. 0+0 counts as 12. A 1, 5, 6 or 12 gives another throw; all values are kept to use in any order.
🚪 First coin leaves base only on a 1. If you have a coin in base and roll a 1 at any point in the turn, that 1 must be used first to bring a coin out — nothing else moves until then, and no other value can bring it out. With no 1 in the turn, a 5 can bring a coin out or move coins.
🔁 A coin may pass over or land on your own coin during the turn, but when the turn ends two of your coins can’t share a normal square, so only moves that allow a valid finish are offered.
➡️ Move along your numbered path to 37, return along the opponent’s path (36→14), then 38 → 39–44 → your inner path to 56.
🔒 Until you capture one opponent coin, none of your coins can move past the opponent’s square 20 on the return route. One capture, by any of your coins, unlocks all your coins.
🏆 If all your coins reach your own 38 safe place while the opponent has never captured, you win at once.
💥 Landing on an opponent on a normal square sends it to base, and you get a bonus throw. ✕ squares block opponents. 44–56 are safe; if Red and Blue meet on 56 both go to base.
🏁 From 56 you need exactly a 1 to enter the Finish circle. Finish all your coins first to win. You must move if a legal move exists.
🥚 Before your first 1, a 5, 6 or 12 only gives another throw and is not kept as a movement value; the first 1 must bring your first coin out.
🏁 Home stretch: when you have a coin on squares 39–56, a value that cannot be used by any coin is simply left unused — the turn only passes when no value you have thrown allows any legal move for any coin.
